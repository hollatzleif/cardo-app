Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026 · Responsible: Leif Hollatz (see contact)

The short version

Cardo is a local-first application: your data lives on your device, in a local database and in files you own. There is no Cardo account, no Cardo cloud, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising — neither in the app nor on this website (which also sets no cookies). We, the developers, never see your content.

What stays on your device

Everything you create: to-dos, notes, calendar entries, habits, health logs, finances, assistant conversations, settings, themes. Cardo stores this in a local SQLite database and in a notes folder on your machine. Nothing of it is transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly enable a feature listed below.

When the app talks to the internet

Every tool that contacts the internet carries a yellow privacy light in the app and explains what it sends before you activate it. The complete list:

  • Update check — Cardo periodically asks our update server (GitHub) whether a new version exists. Transmitted: the app's version number. Configurable in Settings → Updates.
  • Inbox & polls (opt-in) — fetches announcements/polls from our server. Votes are anonymous: only a one-way hash of a random installation id is sent, never anything about you.
  • Weather — sends the city name you type to open-meteo.com to fetch the forecast.
  • Currency converter — sends a three-letter currency code to open.er-api.com, at most once a day.
  • RSS reader — contacts exactly the feed URLs you add, nobody else.
  • AI model downloads — downloads model files from huggingface.co when you set up the local assistant. The models then run entirely on your device.
  • Claude assistant (opt-in) — if you connect your own Anthropic subscription, your assistant messages and workspace files are sent to Anthropic. Cardo shows a consent dialog first; Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that processing.

Sync (opt-in) and end-to-end encryption

Sync is off by default. If you enable it, Cardo encrypts your data on your device (XChaCha20-Poly1305) with a key that only you hold — it is stored in your operating system's keychain and never transmitted. The storage you choose (see below) only ever receives unreadable encrypted blocks. Losing every device and the key means the synced data is unrecoverable — by design, because nobody but you can decrypt it.

Cardo's use of Google Drive (opt-in sync transport)

If you choose Google Drive as your sync transport and sign in with your Google account, Cardo requests exactly one permission: drive.appdata — access to its own hidden application-data folder in your Drive. Concretely:

  • Cardo cannot see, read, or change any of your Drive files. It can only create, read, and delete files inside its own hidden app folder.
  • The only data written there are your end-to-end encrypted sync blocks. Google (and anyone else with access to the storage) cannot decrypt them.
  • Google user data is used solely to provide the sync feature you enabled. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never transferred to third parties, and never used to train AI models.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in your operating system's keychain, on your device only. Disconnecting in Settings → Sync deletes it; you can additionally revoke Cardo's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the app folder's contents from there.

Cardo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

Hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log technical access data (IP address, user agent) to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The forum lives in GitHub Discussions and follows GitHub's terms.

Your rights & contact

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you exercise access, correction, and deletion directly: Settings → Data & backup exports everything; deleting the app (and, if used, the Drive app folder) removes it. For any privacy question, contact kontakt@heike-wagner-hollatz.de.

Cardo is local-first: your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking – this website sets no cookies either.
