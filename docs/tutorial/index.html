Your first dashboard, step by step

Ten minutes from a blank canvas to a board that fits your life. No prior knowledge needed – and nothing you do here leaves your device.

1 · First start: say hi

When Cardo opens for the first time it asks for your name (everything else is optional). This is a purely local profile – it exists so Cardo can greet you, not so anyone can reach you. There is no account and no sign-up.

Right after that, the built-in interactive tour starts and highlights the real buttons on screen. You can skip it at any point and restart it later under Settings → Help → Restart tour.

2 · Edit mode: shape your board

Press Cmd/Ctrl + E (or the button in the top bar) to unlock the canvas. Now you can drag widgets around, resize them at the edges, and remove them with the ✕ in their toolbar. Press Cmd/Ctrl + E again to lock everything in place.

In edit mode a “+ Add widget” button appears – it lists every active tool. Add a To-Do list and a clock to start; you can always rearrange later.

3 · Pages: one board per life area

The tabs in the top bar are dashboard pages – for example “Work”, “Private”, “Focus”. In edit mode, create a new page with +, rename one with a double-click, and delete it with the ✕ on the active tab.

4 · The tool market

Open ⊞ Tools in the top bar. Every tool ships with Cardo and runs locally – “activating” just switches it on. The privacy traffic light shows for each tool what it does with your data: green = fully local, yellow = contacts the internet, always with a plain-language explanation of what exactly would be sent.

5 · The command palette: everything, one keystroke away

Press Cmd/Ctrl + K anywhere. Type what you want – “create task”, “start timer”, “set alarm” – hit Enter, done. Every tool registers its actions here, so you rarely need the mouse.

6 · The tools: more than 45, all local

Every tool ships with Cardo, runs locally and speaks to the assistant. The highlights, by area – the full catalog lives in ⊞ Tools:

  • Assistant – a local AI that turns braindumps into proposal cards and can operate every tool below; optionally powered by your Claude subscription. See Your Assistant.
  • Plan – To-Do & Kanban, Eisenhower matrix, time blocking, projects & milestones, goals/OKRs, calendar, daily routine.
  • Write & collect – Notes (.md files, [[wiki-links]], LaTeX), Files browser, scratchpad, sticky notes, decision log, reading list, bookmarks, code snippets.
  • Focus & time – Pomodoro, workclock, alarm, countdown, clock & world clock (each in several styles), soundscapes, breathing coach.
  • Health & life – habits, hydration, mood journal, sleep log, workout log, meal planner, medication reminders.
  • Money – savings goals, subscriptions & bills, shared expenses with settle-up, currency converter.
  • Learn & utilities – flashcards with spaced repetition, statistics with heatmaps, calculator, unit converter, QR codes, password generator, random picker, colors & palettes, RSS reader, weather.

Most widgets have multiple looks: in edit mode, pick a view (e.g. matrix vs. list) right on the widget frame.

6½ · Sync: your data on every device

Optional and off by default. In Settings → Sync you generate a key (it stays in your system keychain), enter it on your other device, pick a transport – your own Google Drive, any synced folder (iCloud, Dropbox, Syncthing) or a WebDAV server – and Cardo keeps to-dos, notes, settings and assistants in step. Everything is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device: the storage provider only ever sees unreadable blocks. Anyone with your key can read your data, so treat it like a password – and know that losing every device and the key means the synced data is gone, by design.

7 · Make it yours: the design panel

In edit mode, open 🎨 Design. Start with a one-click design preset (a whole look at once), then fine-tune: twenty themes from beloved community palettes (Catppuccin, Nord, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night, Everforest, Kanagawa …), an accent color, fonts and text size, a card style (soft, flat or paper), corner radius, transparency and even a background image. Want more? The theme editor derives your own theme from any palette – all 18 colors are yours to change, export and share. Your personal tweaks survive every theme switch, and whole boards can be exported and imported as files (command palette: “Export board”).

8 · Trust, but verify: the self-test

Settings → Diagnostics → “Run self-test” checks the whole app live – database, every active tool, every command, themes, translations – against a scratch database (your real data is never touched) and gives you a report you can export. If anything ever feels off, run this first and share the report in the forum.

Questions?

The docs answer the most common ones (where is my data? is it really free?). For everything else, the forum is open – no account on Cardo's side needed, just a GitHub login.

Cardo is local-first: your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking – this website sets no cookies either.
