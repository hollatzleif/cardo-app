Your first dashboard, step by step

Ten minutes from a blank canvas to a board that fits your life. No prior knowledge needed – and nothing you do here leaves your device.

1 · First start: say hi

When Cardo opens for the first time it asks for your name (everything else is optional). This is a purely local profile – it exists so Cardo can greet you, not so anyone can reach you. There is no account and no sign-up.

Right after that, the built-in interactive tour starts and highlights the real buttons on screen. You can skip it at any point and restart it later under Settings → Help → Restart tour.

2 · Edit mode: shape your board

Press Cmd/Ctrl + E (or the button in the top bar) to unlock the canvas. Now you can drag widgets around, resize them at the edges, and remove them with the ✕ in their toolbar. Press Cmd/Ctrl + E again to lock everything in place.

In edit mode a “+ Add widget” button appears – it lists every active tool. Add a To-Do list and a clock to start; you can always rearrange later.

3 · Pages: one board per life area

The tabs in the top bar are dashboard pages – for example “Work”, “Private”, “Focus”. In edit mode, create a new page with +, rename one with a double-click, and delete it with the ✕ on the active tab.

4 · The tool market

Open ⊞ Tools in the top bar. Every tool ships with Cardo and runs locally – “activating” just switches it on. The privacy traffic light shows for each tool what it does with your data: green = fully local, yellow = contacts the internet, always with a plain-language explanation of what exactly would be sent.

5 · The command palette: everything, one keystroke away

Press Cmd/Ctrl + K anywhere. Type what you want – “create task”, “start timer”, “set alarm” – hit Enter, done. Every tool registers its actions here, so you rarely need the mouse.

6 · The tools in one minute

  • To-Do – tasks with lists, priorities and due dates. Overdue turns red.
  • Notes – Markdown notes stored as real .md files in a folder you choose. Your files, forever.
  • Calendar – your own appointments with reminders shortly before they start.
  • Pomodoro – focus sessions with breaks; lengths, cycles and sound are all yours to change.
  • Workclock – one button: start when you begin, stop when you rest. Cardo sums up your day.
  • Statistics – completed tasks, work time and pomodoros per day, week or month.
  • Alarm & Countdown – wake-up calls with a chime, and “how many days until…?” at a glance.
  • Quick capture – Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Space from anywhere: thought in, sort it later, send it to To-Do with one click.
  • Daily routine – your morning checklist, fresh every day.

7 · Make it yours: themes

Settings → Theme offers ten looks from beloved community palettes (Catppuccin, Nord, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night …). Pick an accent color override if you like – your choices survive theme switches.

8 · Trust, but verify: the self-test

Settings → Diagnostics → “Run self-test” checks the whole app live – database, every active tool, every command, themes, translations – against a scratch database (your real data is never touched) and gives you a report you can export. If anything ever feels off, run this first and share the report in the forum.

Questions?

The docs answer the most common ones (where is my data? is it really free?). For everything else, the forum is open – no account on Cardo's side needed, just a GitHub login.

Cardo is local-first: your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking – this website sets no cookies either.
