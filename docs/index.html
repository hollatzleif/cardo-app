Docs

Everything you need to get from “installed” to “this is my place now”. Short on purpose – Cardo is meant to explain itself.

Getting started

  1. Install Cardo. Grab it from the download page – one command or one installer, no setup, no account.
  2. Build your first dashboard. Cardo opens on a blank canvas. Press Cmd/Ctrl + E to enter edit mode, then add widgets and place, move and resize them until it feels right. Multiple pages (work, home, focus) are yours to create.
  3. Learn the palette. Cmd/Ctrl + K opens the command palette – the fastest way to add widgets, switch pages or change the theme without hunting through menus.
  4. Let Cardo check itself. In the settings you'll find a self-test button. One click, and Cardo tests itself end to end and shows you a readable report. Run it whenever something feels off.

FAQ

Where is my data?

On your device, and nowhere else. Cardo stores everything in a local SQLite database, and your notes live as plain files in your notes folder – readable without Cardo, and yours to back up however you like. Nothing is uploaded, ever, unless a tool's transparency light is yellow and tells you exactly what it sends and where.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free download, all features included, no trial, no ads. Cardo is funded by voluntary donations that cover the year's running costs – shown transparently as a percentage. Donating changes nothing about what the app can do.

When does sync come?

Sync between your devices is planned as an optional later feature. It will be key-based: you generate a key, your data is end-to-end encrypted with it, and the server can never read anything. It's off by default – if you never turn it on, Cardo stays 100 % offline, exactly as it is today.

What does the traffic light on a tool mean?

Green: the tool works fully local, nothing leaves your device. Yellow: the tool contacts the internet – and it tells you in plain language what it sends, where it goes, and why, before anything happens. There is no hidden third state.

Cardo is local-first: your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking – this website sets no cookies either.
