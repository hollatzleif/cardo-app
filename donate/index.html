Support Cardo

Cardo is free and will stay free. No paywall, no premium tier, no “pro” version. If Cardo gives you a calmer place, and you want to give something back – this is where you can.

Why donations?

Even a local-first app has a few real-world bills: the domain, signing certificates (Apple charges 99 €/year), a small server for the optional sync feature, and a couple of infrastructure tools. Donations exist to cover exactly these costs – nothing more is asked for.

Where we stand for 2026

The honest fine print

We publish only the percentage of costs covered – never absolute amounts, and never who donated. Once 100 % is reached, the barometer disappears and we say so openly: from that point on, further donations go personally to the developer. Nobody should ever feel pressured – Cardo works exactly the same whether you donate or not.

Cardo is local-first: your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking – this website sets no cookies either.
