How voting works

Votes are cast from within the Cardo app (Settings → Polls). Each installation can vote once per poll, identified only by an anonymous device hash – no IP address, no account, no personal data. Results on this page are public.

Cardo is local-first: your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking – this website sets no cookies either.
