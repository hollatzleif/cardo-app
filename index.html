Cardo brings everything into one place.
Arranged the way you want it.

Cardo is a free, freely arrangeable dashboard app for macOS, Linux and Windows: to-dos, notes, calendar, files, Anki-class flashcards, a legal-paragraph book, a local AI assistant and close to 50 tools – as widgets on your personal board, with optional end-to-end-encrypted sync between your devices. Built for everyone who feels overwhelmed and needs one calm place where everything comes together. Your pivot point.

Install (macOS / Linux) 
curl -fsSL https://hollatzleif.github.io/cardo-app/install.sh | sh

macOS (.dmg) Windows (.msi) Linux (.AppImage)

● 100 % local Your data never leaves your device. No account, no forced cloud, no tracking.

🔒 Local-first, for real

Cardo works 100 % offline. Everything lives in an embedded SQLite database on your machine – no server, no account, no telemetry. Zero setup: install, open, done.

🧩 Your dashboard, your rules

Start from a blank canvas. Place, move and resize widgets, keep separate pages for work, home and focus. Make it yours with design presets, themes, card styles and fonts. Cardo adapts to how you think – not the other way around.

🧠 A local AI assistant

Dump your thoughts, get back proposal cards – appointments, to-dos, notes – applied only when you say yes. It runs 100 % on your device. Prefer cloud power? Bring your own Claude subscription instead. Your choice, every time.

📄 Notes & files that stay yours

Write Markdown notes with [[wiki-links]], backlinks and rendered LaTeX, and browse your notes folder – text, images, PDFs – right inside Cardo. Real files in a folder you own, readable with any editor.

🃏 Anki-class flashcards

Real spaced repetition: note types with templates and cloze deletions, the modern FSRS scheduler (or classic SM-2), a card browser and stats – and import or export your existing Anki decks as .apkg. All fully local.

⚖️ Statutes at your fingertips

For lawyers and students: keep the sections you work with, annotated and searchable. Fetch the official text of a § straight from legal sources – Germany, EU, UK, Austria – with its “as of” date, or just type your own. Public statute text, stored on your device.

🔑 Sync with your own key

Optional, off by default: your to-dos, notes, settings and assistants on every device – end-to-end encrypted with a key only you hold. Carried by your own Google Drive, iCloud/Dropbox folder or WebDAV server. Whoever stores it sees only unreadable blocks.

🚦 Transparency traffic light

Every tool carries a privacy light: green means it works fully local. Yellow means it contacts the internet – and Cardo tells you in plain language what it sends and where, before anything happens.

🩺 Checks itself

One button in the settings and Cardo runs a full self-test with a readable report. If something is off, you see exactly what – no guessing, no support tickets.

💙 Free, funded by donations

Cardo is free to download and use. If you like it, a voluntary donation helps cover the year's running costs – that's it. No paywalls, no premium tiers.

The honest part

There is still no Cardo cloud and no account – and that's a feature. Sync is optional and works with a key you generate yourself: everything is encrypted on your device before it travels through storage you choose (your Drive, your folder, your server). Turn it off and Cardo is 100 % offline again. Your data is yours, always.
